# Cleanup replay, 25 November 2026

Prompt `v2` against `v1` on the 38 raw transcripts of run 3
([speed-test-2026-11-25-3.md](speed-test-2026-11-25-3.md)). No speech provider is involved. These are
numbers only. The texts stay on the owner's Mac under `spikes/flowbench/samples/`.

Neither prompt is clearly better. `v1` is faster and steadier. `v2` removes more fillers, but its
model reasons about 4 times longer and it sometimes cuts real words. The cleanup takes under 400 ms at the
median with either prompt, so the speech step is still the bottleneck.

samples and was discarded. Both prompts were then run in batches of 8 transcripts, 65 s apart,
with no 429s. The tool wrote one results file per batch. They are merged here: counts summed,
and the median and 90th percentile recomputed from the per-sample lines.

## Reading of the texts

Claude read the texts. The owner has not judged them yet. There is no gold text yet, so there is no zero-edit rate.

- **Fillers.** Both prompts remove "eh", "pues" and "bueno". `v2` also drops "yeah" and "anyway", and
  some sentence-opening "bueno". It sometimes cuts real content too, such as a leading "for
  example".
- **Self-corrections.** Neither prompt keeps only the corrected version. Both mostly smooth the grammar.
- **Guessed words.** Both prompts sometimes guess. `v1` rewrites more freely: it filled a gap with a
  guessed word, and once turned a statement into a question. `v2` once changed a spoken name into
  a different phrase.
- **Translation and answering.** Neither prompt produced output in the wrong language or answered a question.
  `v1` translated one English phrase inside a Spanish dictation.
