# EU streaming speech, 25 November 2026: summary

Speechmatics `melia-1` on its EU host passed the speed and language part of the gate on all 38
samples. Median 426 ms and 90th percentile 587 ms from key release to cleaned text, against
800 and 1200. Run 3 with OpenAI speech took 1110 and 1290. The final transcript now arrives about 113 ms after
release instead of about 720 ms.

This is not the full gate run. It used 24 Spanish and 14 English samples, not 25 and 25. The
owner has not judged formatting quality or compared it with Wispr Flow. The runs were on a wired network, while run 3
was on Wi-Fi.

## Transcripts

Claude read the texts and compared them with run 3. The owner has not judged them.

- **Speechmatics (run 10):** fewer misrecognitions than run 3, correct Spanish accents, and no
  output in the wrong language or invented foreign words. Small losses:
  - one last word dropped;
  - two words dropped mid-sentence;
  - the English opening of `es-20` dropped. That sample switches from English to Spanish.

  Some names are still wrong ("long chain" for LangChain). Numbers are mixed: "dos 3 millones".
- **Deepgram (runs 7 and 11):** wrong language on `en-01` (only the last four words kept) and `es-20`
  (invented English). Invented words that look Hungarian appear in two Spanish samples. Several
  samples lose their last words, and waiting for CloseStream in run 11 did not change that. Names
  are worse than run 3. English punctuation is cleaner.

## Bugs found and fixed on the way

- Speechmatics sent nothing back after `ForceEndOfUtterance` when no audio was pending, and every
  sample timed out. The session now also sends `EndOfStream` and ends on whichever end message
  comes first. In run 9 `EndOfUtterance` came first.
- Joining `metadata.transcript` across Speechmatics messages glued words together. The text is
  now built from the word list.
